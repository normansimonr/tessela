# Mission Statement: Tessela

## Mission
To detect the textual variants in the Old Testament that carry translational significance.

## Vision
Tessela aims to provide a rigorous, machine-assisted method for identifying meaningful differences between the major ancient textual traditions: the **Masoretic Text**, the **Septuagint**, the **Vulgate**, the **Qumran (Dead Sea Scrolls)** scrolls, and the **Samaritan Pentateuch**. By focusing on "translational significance," we bypass orthographic and phonetic variations to highlight changes that impact the semantic and theological interpretation of the text.

## Core Methodology
The project utilizes semantic decomposition (via Gemini 3.1 Flash Lite) to normalize verses from different traditions into atomic propositions. Differences that persist after this normalization are flagged as translationally significant variants.
